Butter Paper is MIT licensed. It includes the software listed below, each
under its own licence. Packages carry this file as THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md,
generated by `cargo xtask notices` from the exact locked dependency graph of
the build.

## GPUI and GPUI Component

- GPUI: Zed (https://github.com/zed-industries/zed) at
  `8b1497dbd22fb06f5838a7c0b84a1e54fafa71bc`, Apache-2.0, built from
  Butter Paper's fork (https://github.com/apotenza92/zed, branch
  `butter-paper`) with local rendering and test-window fixes.
- Zed's `ztracing` facade is GPL-licensed and is not used: the workspace
  replaces it with an Apache-2.0 shim (`crates/ztracing-shim`) that
  re-exports `tracing::instrument`.
- GPUI Component (https://github.com/longbridge/gpui-component) at
  `c27f5d5c8f70d534978c2f0739ad9e10d4e41eb4`, Apache-2.0, built from
  Butter Paper's fork (https://github.com/apotenza92/gpui-kit, branch
  `butter-paper`) with accessibility, tab, dialog and text-area fixes.

The licence texts of these and every other Rust crate follow under
"Rust crates".

## PDFium

PDFium (https://pdfium.googlesource.com/pdfium, chromium/7881, source

The phone transfer helper is qrcp (https://github.com/claudiodangelis/qrcp,
MIT) with Butter Paper's adapter, built with Go, and serves Signature Pad
5.1.4 (MIT). Their notices follow at the end.

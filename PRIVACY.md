# Butter Paper privacy policy

Butter Paper is a PDF app that runs on your computer. It has no accounts, no
analytics and no advertising, and it does not collect personal information.

## Your documents

Documents, markups, signatures, templates and settings stay on your computer.
Butter Paper never uploads your documents.

## Internet use

Butter Paper connects to the internet only for these features:

- **Signing from your phone.** When you choose to sign with your phone,
  Butter Paper creates a short-lived session on Butter Paper's signature
  relay, and your phone sends the signature image through it. The image is
  encrypted with a key that passes from your computer to your phone only in
  the QR code, so the relay cannot read it. The session and its contents are
  deleted after delivery or within five minutes.
- **Updates (copies downloaded from GitHub).** Butter Paper checks GitHub for
  new releases and downloads updates from GitHub. Copies installed from the
  Microsoft Store are updated by the Store instead and never contact GitHub.

These services see your IP address, as any internet service does.

Incremental boundary

Do not mix two UI frameworks inside one document window.

A GPUI subview inside Electron would still require native surface embedding, event arbitration, focus/IME coordination, accessibility tree composition, and cross-framework layout. That work does not retire the Electron shell and is difficult to test on three platforms.

The safer increment is a separate GPUI executable with a versioned document/page protocol. The first prototype can use a local fixture adapter. A later adapter can share document operations through a process boundary or a deliberately ported Rust core. Keep file mutation in one owner during every milestone.

Performance evidence to collect

Frame pacing

Record input-to-present and missed-frame percentiles at 60 Hz and 120 Hz during pan, scroll, wheel zoom, pen drawing, handle drag, and thumbnail navigation.

Surface transfer

Measure decode, channel conversion, CPU copy, GPU upload, cache hit rate, resident memory, and eviction at fit width and 400%.

Correctness under motion

Compare rapid-motion placeholders and settled detail crops against current output. Verify no stale page or annotation geometry after zoom and recycle.

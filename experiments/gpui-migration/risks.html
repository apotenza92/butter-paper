Evidence-backed risk register

Most uncertainty is below the pixels.

GPUI has credible primitives for a fast custom desktop UI. Butter Paper's decision depends on platform semantics, PDF surface economics, and product correctness. Every critical or high risk below needs a prototype gate before a rewrite commitment.

Go / no-go gates

Gate A · Platform

Inspectable accessibility tree, complete keyboard path, IME corpus, contained transient surfaces, native menus, macOS and Windows parity.

Gate B · Document surface

Hibbeler fit-width and 400% output, virtual thumbnails, 120 Hz traces, bounded copies/uploads/memory, correct rapid-motion settling.

Gate C · Integrity

Rectangle then full tool corpus: selection, handles, properties, undo/redo, save/reopen, import/export, and no coordinate drift.

Gate D · Product shell

Empty/loaded/multi-tab/constrained/transient states at supported sizes, themes, scale factors, and exact keyboard behavior.

Gate E · Distribution

Stable/beta identity, signing, notarization, installers, update trust, N-1 replacement, provenance, rollback, and public requirements.

Decision

Approve a full migration only after A–E pass. Today, approve only A+B as a bounded read-only prototype.

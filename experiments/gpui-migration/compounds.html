Default-first compound policy

Use GPUI directly

  • Actions and key bindings for toolbar commands.
  • Focus handles and tab stops for navigation.
  • Anchored and deferred elements for transient surfaces.
  • Uniform lists for page navigation where row height is normalized.

Keep Butter Paper wrappers

  • Split-button semantics and joined styling.
  • Zoom, fit, page-mode and product icon behavior.
  • Tool registry, shortcut exclusions and contextual properties.
  • Current 32 px targets and Nova density.

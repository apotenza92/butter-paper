Current recommendation

First resolve one coherent, distributable GPUI and GPUI Component graph. Then migrate one Electron contract at a time using the real library API, deterministic behavior tests, a gallery story, and fresh native visual/input proof. Compare the default recreation with Electron/Nova and keep an intentional difference only when its benefit and regression cost are recorded. Do not infer readiness from the historical hand-built shell: tabs, transient surfaces, inputs, accessibility inspection, constrained layout, macOS, Windows, Wayland, packaging, and realistic-document gates remain incomplete.

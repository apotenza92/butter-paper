Compound-scale review

Compose owned GPUI primitives around product semantics.

The compiled milestone uses Butter Paper's owned ButtonGroup and SplitButton for joined zoom, fit, tab-template, Continuous, and Single Page controls. Butter Paper still owns zoom anchoring, shortcuts, closable document tabs, page-layout behavior, product icons, and contextual property state. Current Linux layout evidence passes; matched macOS evidence is blocked.

Component-first compound policy

Use owned raw-GPUI controls

  • ButtonGroup for joined control geometry and related actions.
  • Pressed button state for independent tools and exclusive view choices.
  • Build owned tab and transient-surface primitives only after their full contracts are recorded.
  • Keep focus, disabled state, sizes, Geist, icons, and Nova tokens in one foundation.

Keep Butter Paper domain behavior

  • Two-action split-button semantics when each segment has a distinct command.
  • Zoom, fit, page-mode calculations and meaningful custom icons.
  • Closable, sortable, dirty document tabs and safe-close flow.
  • Tool registry, shortcut exclusions, constrained overflow, and contextual properties.

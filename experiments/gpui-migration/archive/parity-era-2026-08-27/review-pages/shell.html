Thumbnail navigation and multiple tabs

GPUI acceptance target

Virtualized thumbnails must expose the correct page and selected state after recycling. Each tab must retain its own page, zoom, scroll, tool, selection, and dirty state. Closing a dirty tab must enter the existing save/discard/cancel decision without moving state to another document.
